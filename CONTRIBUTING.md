# Contributing

Konnekt is not actively maintained. Pull requests and issues are closed.

**Write to `hello@dpschool.io` before you start anything**, whether a contribution or taking
the project over, and wait for an answer.

## Licence

Contributions are accepted under the [EUPL-1.2](LICENSE) only. By contributing, you confirm
that the work is your own and that you have the right to license it under the EUPL-1.2.

## Checks

Every change passes the project's checks before it is offered: the commands and the rule
"Before you finish" are in [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md), section "Commands", and the linters,
formatters and type checks in the [README's "Code Quality"](README.md#code-quality).
